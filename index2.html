Understanding Giftedness in the Indian Context

It's not just about high grades. Giftedness includes high creative ability, leadership potential, or specific talents in visual/performing arts (PMKVY/NEP Guidelines).

Yes! NEP 2020 explicitly mentions supporting "gifted students/students with special talents" through clubs, olympics, and enriched curriculum.

Fact: Without support, many gifted children disengage, underachieve, or face social emotional difficulties. They need guidance just as much as any other student.
