Understanding Giftedness in the Indian Context

It's not just about high grades. Giftedness includes high creative ability, leadership potential, or specific talents in visual/performing arts (PMKVY/NEP Guidelines).

Yes! NEP 2020 explicitly mentions supporting "gifted students/students with special talents" through clubs, olympics, and enriched curriculum.

Fact: Without support, many gifted children disengage, underachieve, or face social emotional difficulties. They need guidance just as much as any other student.

Teacher Screening Tool

Quickly capture observations about a student and get suggested next steps.

1. Student
2. Traits
3. Classroom
4. Summary

Which traits have you consistently noticed in this student?

How does the student usually behave in your classroom?

This is a quick screening aid, not a formal assessment.

How to use this tool

Use this as a quick reflection before you speak with parents, school counsellors, or your academic coordinator.

  • Complete this after observing the student for at least a few weeks.
  • Where possible, compare notes with another teacher who teaches the same student.
  • Use the summary to decide if you need enrichment, counselling, or formal identification.

You can follow up with our Training & Services or upcoming Events.

Parent Support Planner

Create a simple weekly plan to support your child's strengths at home.

1. About your child
2. Concerns
3. Interests
4. Plan

What are your top concerns about your child right now?

Which areas does your child seem most interested in?

This planner suggests simple weekly actions and is not a clinical recommendation.

Tips for parents

  • Keep the plan realistic: 2-3 small actions per week are enough.
  • Involve your child in choosing activities so they feel ownership.
  • Watch for changes in mood, sleep, and motivation as you try new ideas.
